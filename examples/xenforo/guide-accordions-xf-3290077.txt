[CENTER][SIZE=36px][B]Accordions[/B][/SIZE]

[/CENTER]



[B]Purpose:[/B] The accordion bbcode is designed to give users a sleek alternative to the popular Tabs bbcode and another option to organize their post contents in a friendly manner that also works with all of our other bbcodes.


[accordion]


{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}


{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}


{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}


[/accordion]


The example above is accomplished with the following code:

[CODE]
[accordion]
{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}
{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}
{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}
[/accordion]
[/CODE]

You can add different codes together with |

[SPOILER]

[LIST=1]
[*]number%: Set the width of the accordion box
[*]fleft: block will float left
[*]fright: block will float right
[*]bleft: align the block to the left
[*]bcenter: align the block to the center
[*]bright: align the block to the right
[/LIST]

[LIST=1]
[*]left: title of the slide will be align to the left
[*]center: title of the slide will be align to the center This one is enabled by default for slide titles.
[*]right: title of the slide will be align to the right
[*]open: the slide will be opened by default
[/LIST]


You are welcomed to add as many slides as you want and change their name/contents. However the important thing to note about this addon is the ability to alter their size and the way some of their aspects work.


Another important thing to note is that the above example is set to 100 percent width. To alter the size you have to decrease it.


For example:


[accordion=35%]


{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}


{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}


{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}


[/accordion]


This can be accomplished by



[accordion=35%]
{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}
{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}
{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}
[/accordion]

[CODE][accordion=35%]
{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}
{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}
{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}
[/accordion][/CODE]

You'll notice that I added the 35% tag code to the accordion tag instead of the slides.


You can also do things like move it to the right/left or the center. Here is an example of it being centered.


[accordion=35%|bcenter]


{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}


{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}


{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}


[/accordion]


The code for that as an example is as follows:



[accordion=35%|bcenter]
{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}
{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}
{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}
[/accordion]

[CODE][accordion=35%|bcenter]
{slide=Testing 1}Info{/slide}
{slide=Testing 2}Testing{/slide}
{slide=Testing 3}Stuff{/slide}
[/accordion][/CODE]
