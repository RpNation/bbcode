this is a [B]character sheet[/B] with one scroll box for character details and lots of outfit photos, originally made with high society or slice-of-life roleplays in mind. of course, it isn't limited to that! the icons above the gallery can also be swapped out to make it look more like an inventory or arsenal for characters whose fashion style isn't as important, e.g. for survival roleplays or superpower visuals.
[br][/br][br][/br]
i based the design of this code on the sims character creation screen but tweaked a few aspects to make it easier to work with; that being said, please pay careful attention to the comments i've left throughout to help you when editing the colors/images to suit your needs!
[br][/br][br][/br]
for the [B]transparent pictures[/B], i recommend using [URL='https://www.adobe.com/express/feature/image/remove-background']adobe's free background remover tool[/URL] for the cleanest results without using an editing software. there are also a few RGB values to edit when customizing the colors in this code, which i've marked with a comment towards the top! i highly suggest writing the color names within the comments to help you keep track of what's what.
