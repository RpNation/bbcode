[color=#2980b9][b]Purpose[/b][/color][divide=dotted][/divide]
The Tabs BBCode is used separate out multiple blocks of content into tabs that allow users to cycle through them.

[color=#2980b9][b]Examples & Codes[/b][/color][divide=dotted][/divide]

[CODE]
[Tabs]

[Tab=Tab A]
Content For Tab A.
[/Tab]

[Tab=Tab B]
Content For Tab B.
[/Tab]

[/Tabs]
[/CODE]
